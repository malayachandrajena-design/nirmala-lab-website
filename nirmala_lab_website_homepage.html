About Our Lab

Nirmala Ma'am Lab is dedicated to advanced physics experiments, research activities, and practical learning for students. This website showcases laboratory facilities, experimental setups, and student projects.


Our goal is to encourage innovation, scientific thinking, and hands-on experience in modern physics and materials science.
