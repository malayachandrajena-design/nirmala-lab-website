The Condensed Matter Physics Laboratory, headed by Dr. R. Nirmala, is dedicated to the experimental investigation of strongly correlated electron systems, magnetic materials, and functional oxides.

Our research employs a range of state-of-the-art experimental techniques including SQUID magnetometry, resistivity measurements, heat capacity studies, and X-ray diffraction to understand emergent quantum phenomena in bulk and nanostructured materials.

We collaborate broadly with national and international groups, contributing to the understanding of materials relevant to next-generation electronics, spintronics, and quantum technologies.

Magnetism Frustrated magnets, spin-glass systems, heavy fermions
Crystal Growth Arc-melting, Bridgman, flux growth of intermetallics
Transport Electrical resistivity, Hall effect, thermopower
Thermodynamics Specific heat, entropy, quantum criticality
02 — Research

Research Areas & Publications

⚛️
Strongly Correlated Systems
Investigating heavy-fermion compounds and Kondo lattice systems where electron–electron interactions lead to exotic ground states and quantum phase transitions.
🧲
Frustrated Magnetism
Study of geometrically frustrated magnets, spin-ice materials, and spin-liquid candidates through low-temperature magnetic susceptibility and neutron scattering.
🔬
Functional Oxides
Synthesis and characterisation of perovskite and spinel oxides for multiferroic, magnetocaloric, and colossal magnetoresistance applications.
